      Preferences

      Food search uses Open Food Facts first. If it is down or finds nothing, it falls back to USDA FoodData Central. Without your own free key (fdc.nal.usda.gov) that fallback is limited to a few lookups an hour. The key stays on this device and is left out of backups.

      Local AI (Ollama)

      Describe a meal in plain language. A model on your own PC works out the foods and amounts, and the numbers are checked against USDA FoodData Central (using the USDA key above). Setup steps are in docs/local-llm-windows.md in the repository. The address stays on this device and is left out of backups and GitHub sync.

      History sync (GitHub)

      Keeps your whole history in the data branch of MrtOzdgn/macro-tracker, so it survives clearing the browser and works across devices. The repository is public, so anyone can read this file.

      Create the token at github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new. It is stored only on this device and never written to the repo or to backups.

      Backup

      On a phone, "Back up now" opens the share sheet so you can save the file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files. Elsewhere it downloads the file.
